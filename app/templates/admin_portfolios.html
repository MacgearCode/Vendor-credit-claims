Account managers

A sale's manager: the row for its retailer, brand and item category, else the row for its retailer and brand, else the row for the retailer (every brand), else NetSuite's customer rep. Categories are the item list's (e.g. EUFY Security / Appliances / Baby); targets are set by retailer × brand only, so a category's share of the target is estimated from the last 12 months' sales mix. The manager in force now applies to all history, so a manager sees their whole portfolio (as before).
