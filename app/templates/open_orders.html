Orders

Open orders = NetSuite sales order lines in Pending Approval, Pending Fulfillment or Partially Fulfilled with quantity still to ship (ordered − fulfilled), refreshed from NetSuite about every 30 minutes. A picking line is one such order line. {% if money %}Open value = open quantity × the line's rate, ex GST, in {{ 'NZD' if region == 'NZ' else 'AUD' }} for the region shown.{% endif %} Headline figures are customer orders only: Holding Orders (forward / placeholder orders held for retailers, retailer named in the PO), intercompany orders to MacGear NZ and samples are left out unless ticked above. AU and NZ are never added together. Every open unit is either committed (NetSuite has allocated stock to it: it can be picked, shipped and invoiced now — "ready to ship now") or back-ordered (waiting on stock); the two always add up to the open quantity. In the Top 10 bars the solid part is committed, the faded part back-ordered; "Rank Top 10 by: Ready to ship now" orders them by what the warehouse can actually ship. Committed value = committed units × the line's rate.
