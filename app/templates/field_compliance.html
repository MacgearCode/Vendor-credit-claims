How the % is worked out

  • One count per store × product for the period, using its latest answer. A store visited three times counts once.
  • A blank answer counts as No — skipping a question never helps.
  • Harvey Norman core range: only products HN grades A–D (HN's weekly Mova file), and only at stores whose tier ranges that grade — an AB store is measured on A and B products. Other grades (new, clearing, special order, deleted) never count. A store's tier applies to all three categories (Floorcare, Outdoor Living, Personal Care). HN stores not on HN's tier list (outlets, commercial) aren't measured.
  • JB / TGG merchandising is measured separately: every product asked, except a No the rep marks "not ranged".
  • Next to the graded % (which never goes over 100%): Over and above = HN stores outside the grading that have the product on display this period — what reps secured beyond the grading. Total coverage = of all the HN stores on HN's tier list that reps cover, how many have it on display, using each store's latest answer in the last 90 days (a month alone reaches only about a third of stores). Stores not checked in that time are "unknown", not counted as missing.
  • "Is X still on display" questions (products being cleared) are not compliance.
  • The rep credited is the rep who gave the latest answer.
