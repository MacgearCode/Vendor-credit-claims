How it's worked out (the CYA workbook's method, checked line by line against it). Sales = NetSuite invoice and credit-memo lines on Sales, Freight From Customers, Sales short supply & damage and 3PL income, by transaction month; AU→NZ intercompany left out. Rebate $ = (customer rebate + brand exception + item-category exception + store extra) × gross, at the rates set for each month. Net sales = gross − rebate. Gross profit = net sales − cost of goods (NetSuite estimated cost) − freight − vendor rebates. MDF $ = brand MDF % × gross. Net profit = gross profit − MDF. The GP target is measured against NP % on gross. A PM's (and the total's) target is their brands' targets weighted by gross sales: the NP % they'd make if every brand hit its own target, so it moves with the sales mix. "vs target $" = net profit made − the net profit the targets ask for (target % × gross, brand by brand). Brands with no target are left out of both (marked *). Freight and vendor rebates are NetSuite GL lines by brand (positive = cost, negative = a vendor credit, shown green); they sit on the brand, not on a customer. The numbers keep NetSuite's sign so they tie to the GL; colour shows the effect on profit (red = reduces it, green = adds to it).
