3PL and consignment stock

3PL: location warehouse 3PL, goods Macgear stores for others. Consignment: AU6 Big W and Consignment NZ, stock held at the customer. NetSuite's ageing report carries both at $0, so they are counted in units only, by region; they don't follow the region switch above.

Source. The daily NetSuite Inventory Aging Report: one row per item × location with 13 age buckets, rolled into 0–90 / 91–120 / 121–180 / 181–240 / 241–370 / 370+ with no interpolation — the edges are NetSuite's and nothing is counted twice ("over 120" is the last four bands). Values at average cost, not retail.
Age basis. NetSuite ages stock from its arrival at its current location, so a transfer restarts the clock. Early-2026 NZ stock looks young because Christchurch was emptied into Auckland; part of its rise from May is that stock ageing again.
Regions. AU figures are AUD, NZ figures NZD — never added together.
Excluded. Warehouse 3PL (third-party) and consignment locations (AU6 Big W, Consignment NZ) are in no figure; both are shown separately, in units. MOVA in the Melbourne and Auckland warehouses is Macgear's and is included.
